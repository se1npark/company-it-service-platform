# Teams Audio Issues

## Symptoms

- Teams cannot detect a microphone.
- User can hear audio but cannot speak.
- Browser version works but desktop app fails.
- Issue started after Windows updates.

## First-Line Checks

1. Check Teams device settings.
2. Confirm Windows microphone privacy permissions.
3. Test the headset in Voice Recorder or another meeting app.
4. Check hardware mute buttons and headset firmware.
5. Update audio drivers if the device is not visible to Windows.
6. Clear Teams cache only after device and permission checks.

## Escalate When

- The same headset fails across multiple devices.
- Device Manager shows driver errors after updates.
- The user needs a replacement device.
