# Printer Troubleshooting

## Symptoms

- Printer appears offline.
- Jobs remain stuck in the queue.
- User moved desks or offices recently.
- Printer works for one user but not another.

## First-Line Checks

1. Confirm the printer has power, network, and no paper or toner issue.
2. Check whether other users can print.
3. Clear stuck jobs from the local queue.
4. Re-add the printer from the approved print server.
5. Confirm the user is on the correct network.

## Escalate When

- The printer is unreachable by hostname or IP.
- Multiple users cannot print.
- Vendor maintenance or hardware replacement is required.
