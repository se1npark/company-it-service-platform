# Wi-Fi and LAN Connectivity

## Symptoms

- Device has no internet access.
- Wi-Fi connects but business apps do not load.
- Ethernet works but Wi-Fi fails, or the reverse.
- DNS or gateway errors appear in diagnostics.

## First-Line Checks

1. Confirm whether the issue affects one user or many.
2. Restart network adapter and reconnect to the correct SSID.
3. Test a known-good network cable or docking station.
4. Check IP address, gateway, and DNS values.
5. Compare office network and mobile hotspot behaviour.

## Escalate When

- A whole office area is affected.
- DHCP, DNS, or switch port issues are suspected.
- A firewall or network policy change is involved.
